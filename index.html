01 / Profile

Executive Profile

A clinical consultant whose career developed into hospital-wide leadership across academic affairs, medical education, accreditation, governance and strategic institutional development.

Clinical excellence creates value when it is translated into durable systems, capable teams and measurable institutional progress.Professional positioning

Dr. Adeelah Qattan is a Consultant in Restorative Dentistry and healthcare executive at King Fahd Armed Forces Hospital in Jeddah. She currently serves as Assistant Hospital Director for Academic Affairs and Director of Medical Education and Training while continuing her specialist clinical role.

Her leadership record spans training-system design, institutional and provider accreditation, scholarship planning, academic governance, clinical pathway development, digital transformation and professional development.

Earlier roles include founding and directing the Restorative Dentistry Saudi Board Program at KFAFH, leading the Restorative Dental Division, and serving as Assistant Director of the Dental Department.
